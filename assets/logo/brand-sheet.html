The wordmark

"CYDpod" is set in Source Sans 3 Regular. The "o" is a click wheel drawn to the same size and weight as a real "o", so the word still reads normally at any size. The centre button is the one place CYD yellow appears.

Ring = stem weight

The ring is 16% of the wheel's width, which matches Source Sans Regular stems. It sits on the x-height and baseline, with the same small overshoot as a real o.

Centre button = 25%

The dot is a quarter of the wheel's diameter. It stays yellow on white and graphite, and turns ink on yellow or in one-colour use.

Casing is fixed

Always write CYDpod: CYD in capitals, pod in lowercase. Never cydpod, CYDPod or CYD Pod, in the logo or in text.

The wheel

Lifted out of the wordmark, the "o" becomes the standalone mark. Use it for the app icon, the favicon, a case badge or anywhere the full name won't fit.

Space and size

Leave at least one wheel's width of empty space on every side. Below the minimum sizes, the centre dot fills in, so switch to the mark on its own.
