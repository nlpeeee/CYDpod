# CYDpod 0.5.2

Released 7 November 2026 for the JC2432W328C. 0.5.2 is the power candidate on top of 0.5.1. It adds idle savings and an in-place Sleep, faster Bluetooth reconnects, playlists that open almost instantly (including playlists stored beside the music), and a few smaller fixes. The 0.5.1 baseline is unchanged: compact FLAC, a 4,096-frame PCM ring, flick scrolling and ESP32-A2DP 1.8.12.

## Changes since 0.5.1

**Power and Sleep**
- **Idle with the screen dark:**
  - the panel controller sleeps;
  - touch is read only after the CST820's interrupt, with a short safety poll that catches quick taps until the interrupt has been seen;
  - the main loop runs every 20 ms instead of every 2 ms;
  - the A2DP stream is suspended 10 s after audio stops, and restarts when Play is pressed.
- **Sleep:**
  - starts after 5 minutes with no playback and no touch while the screen is dark, and straight away when the sleep timer runs out;
  - never starts during playback;
  - Bluetooth stops and the chip enters light sleep.
- **Waking:** a touch wakes it, as does typing on the serial monitor. It resumes in place, with no restart: same screen, track and position.
- The CPU stays at 240 MHz. Every saving is a `pwr` setting you can switch at runtime; settings reset at reboot.

**Bluetooth**
- **Faster reconnect:** at boot and after a wake, the saved speaker is called 0.5 s after Bluetooth starts, instead of the library's 10 s wait. The stream start is retried every 300 ms instead of every 10 s. Wake-to-sound was about 22 s on the third board run; the target is about 4 s. `pwr off btfast` restores the old timing.
- **Transmit power:** raised to the ESP32's +9 dBm maximum. `pwr off bttx` restores the controller default.
- **Volume after Sleep:** speaker volume sync is restarted when Sleep stops Bluetooth, so the volume handshake runs again on the reconnect.
- **Resume after a pause:** Play reliably restarts a suspended stream (a race left the resume unqueued).

**Playlists and library**
- **Opening lists:** playlists and Favourites build from three path hashes per track in `/.sys/paths.keys` (about 18 KB), instead of rereading every 712 B catalogue record. A folder is one run of tracks and needs no scan. On the owner's 1,477-track card a list previously took about 4 s and froze the screen.
- **Boot:** the folder and playlist list is reused when the library and the playlist files haven't changed, removing a second 4 s pass.
- **Playlists inside /music:** `.m3u`/`.m3u8` files anywhere in the scanned `/music` tree are playlists too, and their relative entries resolve exactly. Lists in `/playlists` work as before. Two lists with the same name show their folder.
- **One more folder level:** the library indexes `/music/a/b/c/song`. Anything deeper is logged as `catalog: N folders below /music/a/b/c/ not indexed`. Cards with such folders rebuild the catalogue once.
- **Matching change:** two copies whose last two folder names match are no longer told apart by deeper folders. As before, the first copy plays.

**Screen**
- **Portrait Now Playing:** the output chip is gone and the volume slider is full width (166 px, previously 48 px). Audio Output is still available from Options and Settings.
- **Dark-screen taps:** quick taps are no longer missed.

## Provenance

- The release firmware is the exact candidate build compiled from `8c2ed337061f29a1cedcb493da45960768ac71f1`, unchanged and not rebuilt for the version number. `FIRMWARE_VERSION` remains `1.2.0`.
- `v0.5.2` identifies the matching source on `main` plus these release notes.
- Flash binaries, manifests, source, the offline Windows build kit (unchanged from 0.5.1), debug symbols, enclosure v4 and checksums are separate downloads. Use the released binaries for an exact restore.

## Checks

- **Software:**
  - The Classic UI host suites (Bluetooth and wired, including the regenerated portrait geometry), the catalogue suites and the playlist-queue suite (existing M3U fixtures, plus a list in `/music`, no library pass per build, and reuse across boots) all PASS.
  - Both W328C compile variants PASS.
  - Static RAM is 88,400 bytes (Bluetooth) and 88,408 bytes (wired), 1,160 bytes above 0.5.1. Of that, 440 B is the playlist key buffer and state.
  - Evidence: `docs/research/power-candidate.md` and `docs/research/playlist-speed.md`.
- **Owner board reports:**
  - Sleep drew too little for the owner's USB meter to show.
  - After the playlist change the player "feels so responsive".
  - These are owner reports, not timed measurements.

## Not yet confirmed on hardware

- The touch controller's interrupt has not reached the ESP32 in any board log (`int edges=0`). Dark-screen taps and Sleep wakes work through the safety poll, which wakes the chip every 50 ms while asleep.
- Not yet measured:
  - the ~4 s wake-to-sound target;
  - the volume sync after Sleep;
  - the range gain from +9 dBm;
  - the deeper folder level, and whether list 06 now finds its songs;
  - the loop-task stack margin after the deeper scan;
  - `pwr btcycle 20` heap drift;
  - the wired route's behaviour across Sleep.
- A speaker that pages the board itself within 0.5 s of Bluetooth starting has not been seen.
- One audio dropout around a track change and shuffle toggle (`underruns 9`) in the pre-release log has no diagnosis yet. No reboot appeared in the supplied logs; the reported playlist reboot remains unexplained, and free heap fell to 6.4 KB at worst.
- Still open from 0.5.1: AirPods Pro 2, wired hardware and enclosure fit.

## Flashing

Flash the four matching regions to preserve settings and pairings, then verify. Instructions are in the firmware archive README.

The first boot after flashing rebuilds `/.sys/sources.txt` and writes `/.sys/paths.keys`, which takes one library pass. Later boots skip it.

Source and downloads: [CYDpod v0.5.2](https://github.com/nlpeeee/CYDpod/releases/tag/v0.5.2). The repository remains private. Local release: `E:\dev\CYDpod\releases\0.5.2`.
