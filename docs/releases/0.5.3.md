# CYDpod 0.5.3

Released 8 November 2026 for the JC2432W328C. 0.5.3 is about Bluetooth. CYDpod now runs a slimmed-down Bluetooth stack built for it, which frees about 50 KB of memory for streaming. It also keeps speaker volume in step and adopts the speaker's own level on connect, and it has a bigger audio buffer for track changes. Everything else is 0.5.2.

## Changes since 0.5.2

**A Bluetooth stack built for CYDpod**
- Bluedroid, the ESP32's Bluetooth system under ESP32-A2DP, is rebuilt from ESP-IDF v5.5.4 without the parts CYDpod never uses:
  - BLE, BLE Mesh and BluFi;
  - HFP;
  - the dual-mode radio, which now runs Classic only.
- Bluetooth's two internal tasks get 4 KB stacks instead of 8 KB. The logs showed about 6.5 KB of each never used.
- Only 3 of Espressif's 94 rebuilt libraries change (`libbt.a`, `libesp_hid.a`, `libprotocomm.a`), plus the matching `sdkconfig` files. Everything else is Espressif's original binary. How it's built, and what's checked, is in `tools/bluedroid/` and `docs/specs/trimmed-bluedroid.md`.
- **On the board:**
  - free heap when Bluetooth has started rose from 33,848 to 83,896 bytes;
  - while streaming, the largest free block is 34–45 KB, previously 4–6 KB;
  - fixed RAM is 8.7 KB lower and the firmware 306 KB smaller.
- **What it fixes:** with the memory back, the volume link comes up on every connection, and switching from the Xiaomi to the JBL and back works without a power-off. Both failed in 0.5.2 when memory ran low.

**Speaker volume**
- **On connect, CYDpod takes the speaker's own level** instead of sending its own. The speaker reports its volume when CYDpod signs up for volume changes; stock Bluedroid discards that report, and CYDpod's build passes it on (`tools/bluedroid/patches/0001-avrc-ct-forward-volume-interim.patch`). The screen shows the speaker's real level straight away, even after a fresh pairing. A speaker that sends no report gets CYDpod's level after 1 s, as before.
- **Volume stays in step** across slow speaker replies and any order of connection events:
  - a reply that arrives late still counts;
  - a timed-out change keeps the level you chose;
  - the first handshake is retried;
  - the volume link is checked continuously, not only at connect.
- A `stat: vol` line every 10 s shows both sides' levels and the counters behind them.

**Audio**
- The PCM buffer holds 139 ms, previously 93 ms (6,144 frames, 24 KiB), so a slow file open when one track ends and the next begins no longer runs it dry.

**Transmit power (corrected)**
- 0.5.2's +9 dBm setting never took effect: the radio stayed at ESP-IDF's default +3 dBm. 0.5.3 asks for +6 dBm at the point ESP-IDF documents, but the readback still shows +3 dBm (`bt: tx power … max=5`). It's being investigated separately; until then, assume +3 dBm.

## Provenance

- The release firmware is the exact board-tested candidate 4, compiled from `6026ec7b56ecaee2491be30e963c41c72a520b92` on the trimmed libraries. It is unchanged and not rebuilt for the version number. `FIRMWARE_VERSION` remains `1.2.0`.
- `v0.5.3` identifies the matching source on `main`, plus the release tooling and these notes.
- The trimmed libraries are pinned as `bluedroidOverlay` in `dependencies.lock.json`, with the stock and trimmed SHA-256 of all seven changed files, the overlay settings and the patch.
- The offline build kit is 0.5.2's toolchain plus `esp32-libs-3.3.10-cydpod-bt.zip`; `Restore-Toolchain.ps1` applies it after checking every hash. `tools/check.ps1` now refuses to build on stock libraries unless you pass `-StockBluetoothLibs`.

## Checks

- **Software:**
  - Volume host suite PASS, including adopting the speaker's report and the fallback.
  - The ring's index arithmetic was checked on the host at 4,096, 6,144 and 6,000 frames.
  - Both W328C compile variants PASS.
  - Static RAM: 79,652 bytes (Bluetooth) and 79,660 bytes (wired), 8,748 bytes below 0.5.2.
  - Evidence: `docs/research/bluedroid-trim.md` and `docs/research/bt-volume-robust.md`.
- **Owner board runs (JBL Flip 3 and Xiaomi Sound Pocket):** boot, streaming, speaker switching without a power-off, a fresh pairing, Sleep and wake, and volume adoption on both speakers all passed. These are owner runs with serial logs, not timed measurements.

## Not yet confirmed on hardware

- **Transmit power:** it still reads +3 dBm (above).
- **Occasional JBL dropouts:** these come from the radio link. Bluetooth stops sending for 0.2–0.9 s while CYDpod's buffer stays full. They were seen mostly soon after a reconnect, and once in a window that included a screen wake. They didn't recur in the latest short runs, but aren't fixed.
- **Lowest free heap:** 21 KB, once, reconnecting to the JBL after Sleep.
- **A track-change underrun:** one log showed `underruns 2` around quick manual Next and seek presses.
- **Still open from 0.5.2:** the touch interrupt, the ~4 s wake-to-sound target, AirPods Pro 2, wired hardware and enclosure fit.

## Flashing

0.5.3's boot files are byte-identical to 0.5.2's. From 0.5.2 or later, flashing `app.ino.bin` at `0x10000` is enough. Otherwise flash all four regions. Settings and pairings are kept either way; instructions are in the firmware archive README.
