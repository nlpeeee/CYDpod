# CYDpod 0.5.5

Released 8 November 2026 for the JC2432W328C. 0.5.5 adds a 5-band equalizer and makes Bluetooth earbuds work far better: full-quality audio sent in full packets, a bitrate that adapts to the link, and Play that reconnects earbuds that have dropped the link.

## Changes since 0.5.4

**5-band equalizer**
- **Bands:** 60 Hz, 250 Hz, 1 kHz, 4 kHz and 14 kHz, ±12 dB each, on their own Equalizer page, opened from Settings → Playback and from Now Playing → Options.
- **Presets:** Flat, Bass, Vocal, Treble and Loudness.
- **Clean when boosted:** the gain is loudness-matched and a limiter catches peaks, so boosts stay clean.
- **Smooth changes:** changing a band glides the level.
- **Fixed-point:** the filters are biquads with an Xtensa inner loop. On the board, five active bands cost 360–400 cycles per frame, 0.6–0.7% of one core at 44.1 kHz.
- **No cost when off:** with the EQ off or flat, audio passes through bit-exact.
- Details: `docs/specs/eq-5band.md`, `docs/research/eq-5band.md`.

**Bluetooth: earbuds and full quality**
- **Full packets.** CYDpod now fills every A2DP packet before sending it: 8 SBC frames at 328 kbps on a 1008-byte link, 9 at 292 kbps. Previously each 30 ms tick also sent a small leftover packet. On the AirPods at 328 kbps that is about 436 packets per 10 s instead of 668, with the same audio, an empty send queue and a steady 54–59 KB of free heap.
- **Adaptive SBC rate.**
  - Every connection starts at full quality, 328 kbps (bitpool 53).
  - If the link backs up, the rate steps down one notch at a time (292, 257, 229, 200, 165 kbps), changing mid-stream without reconnecting.
  - It climbs back to 328 after 60 s of clean streaming.
  - Speakers stay at 328.
- **Play reconnects.** Earbuds often end the link a few seconds after a pause. Pressing Play now pages them straight away and the music starts when they connect.
- **Restarts and idle wait.**
  - If the earbuds suspend the stream while you are playing, CYDpod starts it again, at most twice in 30 s.
  - A device that ends the link soon after an idle pause gets 60 s before the next idle suspend.
- **Quiet UI.** While a link is backing up, the Now Playing marquee and list flings hold still. That frees the radio, and they come back as soon as the link is clean.
- **Clearer logs.**
  - `bt: switched to "X" (it connected)`.
  - `bt: authenticated "X" (already paired)` for a known device; `bt: paired with` now means a new pairing.
  - A `stat: a2dlink` line every 10 s gives the rate, bitpool, frames per packet, link mode, signal and queue counters.
- **Serial commands:** `btd`, `btd rate auto|<kbps>`, `btd pack on|off`, `btd ui auto|quiet|on`, `btd txpwr`, `btd pkt`, `btd qos`, `btd forget`.
- Details: `docs/specs/bt-rate-and-earbuds.md`, `docs/research/tws-earbuds.md`.

**Bluetooth stack**
- Two new patches to the trimmed Bluedroid:
  - 0002: link counters and runtime SBC rate;
  - 0003: full packets.
- Only `libbt.a` and `versions.txt` change against 0.5.4's trimmed package. The overlay is re-pinned in `dependencies.lock.json`.

## Provenance

- The firmware was compiled from `010c6f6` on the trimmed libraries with patches 0001–0003. `FIRMWARE_VERSION` remains `1.2.0`.
- `v0.5.5` identifies the matching source on `main`. The changes after the firmware commit are documentation only.
- The build kit is 0.5.4's toolchain and A2DP overlay plus the new trimmed-Bluedroid overlay.

## Checks

- **Host:**
  - UI and catalog suite PASS (65 suites, with the Equalizer page tests);
  - Bluetooth policy, rate policy and volume suites PASS;
  - EQ core suite PASS (response against float, limiter, bit-exact bypass, presets).
- **Release compile:** `tools/check.ps1 -Board jc2432w328c -SkipProbe -SkipHostMedia -LargePcmRing -PcmRingFrames 6144` PASS, both images built.
  - Static RAM: 80,852 bytes (Bluetooth) and 80,860 bytes (wired).
  - Flash: 1,327,885 and 1,328,025 bytes.
- **Owner board runs (development builds):**
  - the EQ, presets and Equalizer page;
  - AirPods Pro 3 at 328 kbps with full packets (`fpp=8.0`, no queue build-up);
  - FreeBuds Pro at their own 229 kbps maximum;
  - JBL and Xiaomi.

  These are owner runs with serial logs, not timed measurements. This exact release image was assembled from those builds plus the start-at-328 and animation changes, and has not been run on the board as a whole.

## Not yet confirmed on hardware

- **FreeBuds Pro reconnects:** they sometimes end the link about 3 s after the stream starts, in sessions where the remote-control channel (AVRCP) does not come up.
- **AirPods silence once:** one session went silent while the link stayed up, after the rate stepped down mid-stream. Later mid-stream changes were fine.
- **MP3 seeking:** long seeks into MP3 files take several seconds, because MP3 seeking replays the file from the start.
- **Startup transmit power:** the +6 dBm power still reads back as +3 dBm at startup (`btd txpwr` sets and reads back correctly).
- **Still open from 0.5.4:** the touch interrupt, the ~4 s wake-to-sound target and enclosure fit.

**Confirmed after release:** the owner ran a wired I2S DAC from the Bluetooth build (Settings → Audio Output → Headphones), and it works. The separate wired-only image is retired from the next release.

## Flashing

0.5.5's boot files are byte-identical to 0.5.4's. From 0.5.2 or later, flashing `app.ino.bin` at `0x10000` is enough. Otherwise flash all four regions. Settings and pairings are kept either way; instructions are in the firmware archive README.
