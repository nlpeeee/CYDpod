CYDpod / Design proposal

Classic menus. Direct touch.

The familiar grey header, blue selection and focused music hierarchy, adapted for tapping and dragging on the CYD. Try Music → Songs → a track, then Back.

What carries the resemblance

Menus with memoryBack returns to the same list position.
Touch the item itselfFull-width rows; drag long lists. No virtual wheel.
Focused Now PlayingLarge transport controls. Volume has its own adjustment screen.
Honest capabilitiesProgress is read-only until M4A seek works. Output routes reflect firmware support.

Interactive design concept only. Sample music, tags, times and connection state; no audio or device control. Artist, Album and Song browsing are required for the first usable portrait UI. Browser behaviour does not establish device usability, playback performance or memory use.
